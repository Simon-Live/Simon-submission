# Privacy Policy (Track C draft, fact input for the lawyer)

Draft of 3 November 2026. Not reviewed by a lawyer. Every data fact matches the app's own wording (web/consent.js, web/model/me.js, part7/auth.py).

This policy explains what Simon collects, why, who it goes to, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

## Who is responsible

Simon is run by Simon Live LLC, a New York company, whose team is Mo Alborati and Shruti Doshi. For anything about your data, email [hello@simon.live](mailto:hello@simon.live).

## What we collect

- Videos you record or upload, and for trainer recordings their sound when you allow the microphone.
- What we make from them: 2D and 3D body positions, 3D body meshes, your score and feedback, and short clips of your takes.
- If you sign in (optional): your email address, which takes and movements are yours, and your choice to keep takes for 90 days.
- What you choose to add: a name on a challenge board, notes to a trainer, and feedback you send through the Feedback tab.
- Technical data: your IP address, used to limit and protect the service, a sign-in cookie, error reports from your browser, and our server logs.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

## How we use it

- To analyse your movement and show your result, score and feedback.
- To sign you in and keep the takes you chose to keep.
- To run features you use: sharing a result, a challenge board, a trainer's class view.
- To keep Simon secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

## Who it goes to

Analysing a movement needs AI models we do not run ourselves. These services receive only what each step needs:

- Meta's Model API: frames of your video, so SAM 3.1 can find the people in it; a smaller copy of a trainer recording, so Muse Spark can name its poses; your video and the trainer's video when a second opinion is asked for; one frame and a grey body figure when you ask Muse Image to draw you in the corrected pose; and the speech in a trainer video, so Muse Voice Transcribe can write its spoken cues. Meta's terms say it does not use content sent to SAM 3.1 to train its models.
- Baseten, which runs Muse Spark for Simon: the wording of your feedback (which joint, how far off, when), to phrase it.
- Cloud GPU providers, such as Modal, when your take is processed there: your video, to build the 3D bodies. They process it for us and keep nothing after the job.
- Our hosting, network and email providers, to serve the app and send sign-in mail.

If you turn on Live check while recording, your browser downloads Google's MediaPipe code and model from a public CDN. The live check itself runs on your device, and no frame of your video leaves it for this.

Some things are visible to others because you choose them: anyone with a result's link can open it; a challenge board shows the names and scores of its entries; and a trainer's page shows the scores and most common cues of the takes made on their movement, without names. Private takes are left out.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety or the service.

## How long we keep it

- Takes, their videos, meshes and results: 7 days, then they are deleted automatically.
- Takes you chose to keep while signed in: 90 days.
- A trainer recording with its sound and meshes: 7 days. A movement published to the catalog stays until its trainer deletes it or asks us to.
- A challenge board entry keeps its name and score for as long as the challenge runs.
- Your account: until you delete it. Sign-in sessions last up to 30 days.
- Server logs: only as long as we need them for security and fixing problems.

## Your choices and rights

You can delete a take from its result page at any time, and a trainer can delete their recording from its page.

When signed in, Delete my data on the [Me](#/me) page removes your account, your sessions, the takes you kept and the link between you and your movements.

Depending on where you live, including California (CCPA/CPRA) and the EU and UK (GDPR), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Email [hello@simon.live](mailto:hello@simon.live) and we will answer within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Deleting from Simon cannot undo what was already sent to Meta's Model API for analysis.

## Children

Simon is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has used Simon, email [hello@simon.live](mailto:hello@simon.live) and we will delete their data.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), keep keys and passwords out of our code and logs, and limit who can reach the servers. No service is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee it, but we will tell affected users and the authorities if a breach puts your data at risk, as the law requires.

## Where your data is processed

Simon is run from the United States, and the services above may process data in the United States and other countries. Where the law requires it, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer for international transfers.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top, and we say so on the app if the change is significant.
