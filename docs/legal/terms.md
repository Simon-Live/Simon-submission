programme after an illness or injury.

measurements. The measurements can be wrong. An AI model phrases some sentences of your feedback, and Meta's AI models give second opinions, answers and reports. The result labels a sentence an AI model phrased as AI where it shows that sentence; a sentence the voice speaks is not labelled as it is said. They can be wrong too. None of them changes your score.
