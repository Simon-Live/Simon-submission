# Design documents

Copies of the working specs and architecture decision records (ADRs) kept in the maintainers' workspace,
for anyone building or debugging Part 5 from this repository. Host paths, key locations and workspace
tooling are neutralised. Where a copy and the code disagree, the code, `CONTRACT.md` and `docs/schemas.md` win.

`specs/` and `adr/` are generated by `scripts/make_design_copies.py` from the workspace sources (`make design-copies`
rewrites them, `python3 scripts/make_design_copies.py --check` exits 1 when one differs) and are not edited by
hand. Only this index is. The script needs the workspace sources, so it runs in the maintainers' workspace.

Paths such as `staging/`, `tasks/`, `progress/`, `qa/`, `docs/assumptions/`, `STATUS.md` and `scripts/` files other
than `scripts/make_*.py` and `scripts/sync_contracts.py` belong to that workspace and are not in this repository.
Specs written before ADR-0012 describe refusals and exit 3; ADR-0012 replaced every refusal with a warning and an F cap.

Start with `docs/dev/DEVELOPING.md`.

## Specs

- [`part5-fault-analysis.md`](specs/part5-fault-analysis.md): the master spec: pipeline, knee valgus, movement guard, repetitions, visibility, phrasing registry, CLI.
- [`journey-followup.md`](specs/journey-followup.md): time per fault, screen direction, the replay file, the score and live AI phrasing.
- [`check-5.md`](specs/check-5.md): `make check-5`: the held-out gate and the reported slices.
- [`calibration.md`](specs/calibration.md): calibration on seed 5, the frozen rules and the held-out attempts.
- [`integration-contract.md`](specs/integration-contract.md): the schemas, the compatibility table, `part5 contract`, `part5 doctor`, `make check-5-contract`; Part 7's no-person analysis (`source`, `no_person`) and `score.uncapped` (schema 1.9.0).
- [`real-takes.md`](specs/real-takes.md): `make report-real`: Part 3's kit, Part 2's takes, stock and multi-rep real takes.
- [`viewer.md`](specs/viewer.md): the 3D replay page in `web/`, the fault list and the joint highlight.
- [`staging.md`](specs/staging.md): the analysis API served by `part5 serve`; its staging stack is maintainers' tooling and not in this repo.
- [`neighbour-check.md`](specs/neighbour-check.md): maintainers' tooling, not in this repo: the chain through Parts 2, 3 and 4.
- [`oneshot-watch.md`](specs/oneshot-watch.md): maintainers' tooling, not in this repo: relaunching an unattended run.
- [`simon-publish.md`](specs/simon-publish.md): maintainers' tooling, not in this repo: how files reach this repository.
- [`second-opinion.md`](specs/second-opinion.md): Muse Spark's second opinion on both clips (`second_opinion`), lines that repeat no phrase, and the follower's language (`--lang`).
- [`history-opinion.md`](specs/history-opinion.md): `part5 history-opinion` and `POST api/history-opinion`: Muse Spark's second opinion over a series of analyses, observations that each cite a take and a second, a guard that drops what it cannot ground; built after the candidate and not in it.
- [`ask-this-take.md`](specs/ask-this-take.md): `part5 ask` and `POST api/ask`: one question about one analysed take, answered by muse-wonder-1.0 with tools that read only that take (`seek` and `spotlight` move the player), every number in the answer found in a tool result or the measured facts in its place; the contract Part 7 reads (`simon.ask.v1`).
- [`score-v2.md`](specs/score-v2.md): `simon.score.v2`: misses weighted by the trainer's range, a ceiling of 89 for a take that reports a fault (`score.fault_cap`), schema 1.10.0.
- [`score-v3.md`](specs/score-v3.md): `simon.score.v3`: v2's weight and its ceiling of 89 for one reported fault, and a ceiling of 79 for two or more (`score.fault_cap` is the ceiling in force), schema 1.11.0.
- [`foot-keypoints.md`](specs/foot-keypoints.md): heel and toe points read from Part 2's pose3d.json as an optional side input, the `foot_rotation` and `ankle_inversion` measures, when they are hidden (a foot that jitters, the far foot of a side view, is not measured: attempt 5), their cues and how they are calibrated.
- [`foot-keypoints-attempt-5.md`](specs/foot-keypoints-attempt-5.md): the record of attempt 5 split out of the foot spec: the ruling, the failure on seeds 37 and 67, the candidates, the jitter rule, its seed 5 evidence and its costs and known limits.
- [`profiles.md`](specs/profiles.md): analysis profiles `dance`, `rehab` and `fitness`: the profiles file, the loosen-only rule, tempo, schema 1.7.0 and the seed 5 report (values set by hand).
- [`near-misses.md`](specs/near-misses.md): `diagnostics.near_misses`, the candidate faults detection considered and dropped and why (schema 1.8.0); additive, nothing reads it.
- [`quality.md`](specs/quality.md): `quality`, how well the camera saw the follower's take (schema 1.13.0): a level, reasons, five raw signals and a score cap of 59 that is reported and not applied; limits in their own file, targets written before they were set, and what seed 5, seed 73 and Part 7's dark take read.
- [`history-per-rep.md`](specs/history-per-rep.md): for comparing takes with each other, schema 1.12.0: a fault's size in each rep that found it (`rep_sizes`), each rep's tempo ratio in `score.per_rep`, and the margin of a near miss past its threshold (`margin_deg`); additive, nothing reads them.
- [`score-credit.md`](specs/score-credit.md): `score.credit`, the DDR-style credit display beside the score (schema 1.13.0): a percent and points earned per measure and frame by how close the follower stayed, counted on the example's clock, reported only; the grade of the credit is informational.
- [`body-mask.md`](specs/body-mask.md): score the body you have (schema 1.14.0): a declared mask (`seated`, or not using an arm, a forearm or a leg) drops every measure that reads a masked joint from detection and the score, after alignment; `body_mask` lists the scored and the not scored chains.
- [`guard-cross-person.md`](specs/guard-cross-person.md): research lane (TICKET-101, stopped at step 4, nothing wired): the movement guard on correct takes by another person, the cause measured on REHAB24-6 and seed 5, an opt-in `--cross-body` population for the synthetic generator, and the reproduction test the construction failed.
- [`language-cues.md`](specs/language-cues.md): the cues and reasons of an analysis in the follower's language (schema 1.14.0): one batched model call after the fault lines, a word test per item with the English text kept and the reason when it fails, `phrasing.cues[]` naming the backend and model of each, and `phrasing.native_review`, false until a native reader checks a language.
- [`rep-persistence.md`](specs/rep-persistence.md): attempt 4: persistence judged in each rep of the example, rep by rep for an example of two or more reps (each follower rep against the example rep that matches it, foot measures included), a side lean that does not wrap, and the seed 5 record per part.
- [`scoring-validity.md`](specs/scoring-validity.md): `make bench-score`: planted joint-angle errors of 0 to 30 degrees through the pipeline (score and first-fire angle per joint), the skeleton-level invariance table (frame rate, mirror, trim, translation, yaw, scale) and the score properties; the video-level invariances are Part 7's.
- [`measure-branch-cuts.md`](specs/measure-branch-cuts.md): TICKET-094: the four measures read with `arctan2` (`upper_arm_forward`, `thigh_forward`, `trunk_lean_forward`, `head_forward`) do not jump a turn where their branch cut falls; closed along the clip and put on one branch by the clip's median; every other measure audited; no output change.
- [`own-reference-range.md`](specs/own-reference-range.md): draft, stopped on seed 5 by its own clause (no clean fit): the range-of-motion check against the follower's own correct rep (`r = ln((span_follower + t) / (span_reference + t))` per measure the reference moves by a threshold), its targets written before any seed 5 number, the own-reference pairing of the gate slice, the seed 5 fit and its stop clause; nothing is wired into the pipeline.
