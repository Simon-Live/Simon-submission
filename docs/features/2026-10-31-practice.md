## R7 Adaptive difficulty (TICKET-077)
Added: "Learn in sections" on the follow screen. Sections come from the Part 1 keyframes repetitions, else equal slices
of about 4 s. A ladder takes the person through each section slow then at full speed, the sections joined, and the
full routine; a step passes at 80. The reference video loops the step's range at the step's playbackRate. Each take
sends its `section`; the server cuts the trainer clip to that range (stretched by 1/speed for slow steps) so Part 5
compares like with like.

- Files: `web/practice.js`, `web/practice.css`, `web/reference.js` (setSection, preview, sectionTime), `web/follow.js`,
  `web/take-flow.js`, `part7/sections.py`, `part7/server.py`.
- Routes: `POST api/follow` takes `section {start_ms, end_ms, index, count, first?, speed?}`; `GET api/jobs/<id>`
  returns it. `POST api/group` ignores it.
- Test ids: `practice`, `practice-toggle`, `ladder`, `step-<key>` (`s1@slow`, `s1@full`, `j1-2`, `full`),
  `practice-watch`, `practice-now`, `practice-pending`, `practice-done`.
- Tests: `tests/part7/test_practice.py` (frame range, rebasing, slow stretch, bad ranges give 400),
  `tests/part7/practice.unit.test.mjs`, `tests/part7/practice.browser.mjs` (ladder unlock, playbackRate, loop,
  locked step refused), `staging/checks-practice.mjs`.
- Try it on staging: Follow a movement, tick "Learn in sections", watch step 1 loop at the slow speed, record, and
  come back to the Follow screen after the result to see the step pass at 80.
- Limits: progress is per browser. A movement with a 3D figure instead of a video cannot loop a section. The score is
  counted when the person returns to the Follow screen, not pushed from the result screen.

## Contract deviations
- `section` has two optional extra fields, `first` (joined steps) and `speed` (slow steps).
- The cut runs in the request (`server._follow`), not inside `follow_job`, so a bad range is a 400.
- `mode` also reaches `chain.follower` and `group_job`, to pass `--profile`.
- The trainer's group job sends the stored mode; there is no picker on the Record screen.
