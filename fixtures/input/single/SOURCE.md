# Single-person fixture — source

- **Clip:** "Side View of a Woman Stretching in a Health Club", Pexels video 6019962
  <https://www.pexels.com/video/side-view-of-a-woman-stretching-in-a-health-club-6019962/>
- **Licence:** Pexels free licence (free to use, no attribution required; attribution given here anyway).
  <https://www.pexels.com/license/>
- **Original file:** `6019962-hd_1080_1920_25fps.mp4` (1080×1920 vertical, 25 fps, 22.0 s), downloaded 2026-10-27.
  The video itself is not in this repo (`*.mp4` is gitignored); re-download from the page above.
- **Frames here:** sampled at 5 fps (111 frames) with `part2 frames`, downscaled to 720×1280,
  JPEG quality 85. `frames/frames.json` holds `frame` / `t_ms` per frame.
- **Content:** one woman in true side profile against a plain studio background, seated
  cross-legged stretches (arms overhead/behind, forward reach). The profile view is the
  point: the one-person done criterion requires labelled frames including side views,
  and this clip is side-on throughout.
- **Known limits:** seated poses throughout (no standing/walking), loose trousers make
  knee/ankle clicks approximate, and nose→ankle span is short when seated, which makes
  the 5%-of-height reprojection bar strict. Chosen by the team's instruction to use
  found open-licensed footage instead of waiting for filmed recordings.
