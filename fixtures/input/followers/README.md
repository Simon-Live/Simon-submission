# Dummy follower takes (synthetic, labelled)

Generated by `make dummy-data` (`scripts/make_dummy_followers.py`, seed 5, deterministic) from
`fixtures/input/expected/` (Part 4 commit `dc3d9f2`, see `fixtures/input/expected/SOURCE.md`). This is the
**tuning set**: every threshold and guard limit is chosen on it and nothing else (`docs/specs/calibration.md`).
The gate reads a different set, `fixtures/input/heldout/`, made by `make heldout-data SEED=<n>` (29, then 31,
then 37). Stand-in until real follower `skeleton.json` files exist upstream (#12).

**Format.** Each `<set>/<take_id>/skeleton.json` uses Part 4's `simon.animation.v1` shape (same keys,
joints and coordinate frame). Every frame has `interpolated: false`, and `bones[].length_m` is the median
length measured on that take. the team decided on 2026-10-28 (#18) that the follower file uses the
same shape as the expected clip.

**Takes.** 2 sets × 3 camera angles (front 0°, 45°, side 90°) × 2 lighting conditions (good, low) × 9 takes:
2 correct, 6 with a single fault (one per type, `knee_caving` included), 1 with two faults (never
`knee_caving`). That is 108 takes in total (24 correct, 96 labelled faults).

**How a take is made:**
1. Rescale the body uniformly (0.9–1.1).
2. Inject the faults, rotating limb chains so bone lengths are kept.
3. Add natural variation of about 1–2° (not a fault).
4. Warp the timing: speed 0.8–1.25 and an idle lead-in of 0–15 frames.
5. Add estimator noise that drifts over a few frames (AR(1)). Depth error is larger than in-plane error,
   low light roughly doubles it, and joints on the far side from the camera are noisier.
6. Re-centre on the hip midpoint.

| fault type | label measure | sides | size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| arms_wider | upper_arm_direction | both / left / right | 15–25° |
| foot_out | thigh_direction | left / right | 12–20° |
| knees_shallow | knee_flexion | both | 35–50% less bend |
| elbow_bent | elbow_flexion | left / right | 25–40° |
| trunk_lean | trunk_lean_forward | center | 15–25° |
| knee_caving | knee_valgus | left / right | 15 to 25° |

`knee_caving` rotates the knee about the hip to ankle axis toward the midline, so both bone lengths stay.
Its size is `knee_valgus` (ADR-0004: how far the knee sits inside the hip to ankle line, as an angle). Where
the knee is barely bent the knee cannot go that far, so the label's `magnitude_deg` (the median over the
full-strength window) is lower than the drawn size, most on the step touch.

**Labels.** `labels.json` names the generator `seed` and holds one entry per take: angle, lighting,
`variant` (`clean`, or `dropout` for the copies below), `label_source` (`synthetic`), `correct`, body scale,
speed, lead-in, and `faults[]`. Each fault has `type, measure, body_part, joints, side, direction`, plus
`magnitude_deg` (the median change over the full-strength window, measured before noise) and
`peak_deg`. It also has `text`, `follower_frames` and `expected_frames`.

**Dropout variant (#25).** `python3 scripts/make_dummy_followers.py --dropout-variant` adds, for every side and
45° take, a copy whose id ends in `_dropout` (`variant: "dropout"`, same faults). Each frame has a per-joint
`conf` (lower on the far side and in low light) and a few bursts of 3 to 8 frames where a far-side elbow,
wrist, knee or ankle is `null` or has `conf` below 0.3; the label's `dropouts[]` lists them. The clean
takes are the same bytes with or without the flag. This seed 5 set has no dropout copies; the held-out sets
do (`make heldout-data`), and `make check-5` reports them as `dropout/<angle>` slices, never gated.

`--seed N` and `--out DIR` choose the seed and the folder (default `fixtures/input/followers`).
