# The judges' sample movements

Four movements made from openly licensed stock clips with Simon's own trainer pipeline (Parts 1 to 4, then Part 6),
through the judges' relay, on Nov 4, 2026. `deploy/judges/run.sh` copies them into the local catalog beside Part 6's
eight built-in samples. Each `manifest.json` names its source in `description`.
